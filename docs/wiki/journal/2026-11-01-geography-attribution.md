# Geography data attribution

`geonames.json` is derived from [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/) and licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The source data was downloaded on 17 September 2026. Data is supplied without warranties of accuracy, timeliness or completeness.

The retained offline dataset comes from the archived app. Country and place names resolve discovery locations without sending the profile to a geocoding service. `resources/geography/zone1970.tab` maps country codes to IANA timezones; `tzdb-LICENSE` and `tzdb-version.txt` retain its license and source version. Current daylight-saving offsets come from the runtime's Intl data. Unknown locations do not exclude a posting.
